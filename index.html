Our Story

Locally owned. Eco-friendly. Community-first.

Studio B Salon & Spa provides skincare, spa services, hair salon services, advanced color techniques, and precision cutting to the Tri-State area (MD–PA–WV). We are a locally-owned small business that converted an older building into an eco-friendly environment — and we're proud supporters of local charities in our community.

Meet the Team

Best in beauty, from exceptional people.

When you visit Studio B, you'll receive the best in beauty services from a team of exceptional professionals — stylists, nail technicians, estheticians, and massage therapists who love what they do.

Cristy Tritle

Owner / Master Senior Stylist

Licensed since 1989

Cristy opened Studio B in 2002 and worked behind the chair as a cosmetologist for 36 years. Though she has retired from hairdressing, she enjoys managing the salon and ensuring staff and clients experience the best salon and spa environment possible.

Beth LeHardy

Senior Cosmetologist

Licensed since 1991 • Joined 2015

Beth's love for her career started as a little girl, following in her grandmother's footsteps. Her positive and upbeat personality is undeniable — passionate and talented, whether you want hairstyling, coloring, texture, natural nails, or a head-to-toe makeover.

Michael Elliot

Hair Stylist

Licensed since 1991 • Joined 2015

Michael is an artist who loves transforming clients' hairstyles and colors, always up for a new challenge to showcase his creativity. He works with any length of hair and is especially talented in precision cutting for women's bobs and men's haircuts.

Jessica Atha

Senior Cosmetologist

Licensed since 2008 • Joined 2013

Jessica is passionate about the salon industry — hair styling, coloring, texturing, natural nails, full-body waxing, facials, and makeup. She enjoys cutting and styling textured hair and loves color work with a focus on blondes and fashion colors.

Tricia Drury

Hair Stylist

Licensed since 2015 • Joined 2020

Tricia's sweet and friendly personality is relaxing for her clients. She loves doing long hair, foiling, and balayage that allows her to show her creativity — and specializes in men's haircuts and fades.

April McAllister

Cosmetologist

Licensed since 2009 • Joined 2024

April graduated from Washington County Technical High School in 2009, took a break to embrace life as a mother, and missed making people look and feel their best. She offers all hair services, natural nails, full-body waxing, facials, and makeup.

Abigail Francella
